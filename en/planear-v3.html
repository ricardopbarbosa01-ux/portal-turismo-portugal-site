    How it works

    1. 1Tell us what you want

      Interest, region, dates, travellers and style. Five taps, no sign-up.

    2. 2Get your plan instantly

      We pick the best base to stay and bring together places to stay with live prices, experiences and a car.

    3. 3Book with our partners

      Every link opens with your dates and travellers already filled in. You pay the partner directly, at the same price.

    How much does the planner cost?

    Nothing. The planner is free and needs no account. When you book through our links, the partner pays us a small commission — your price does not change.

    Are the prices real?

    Yes. The accommodation map shows partners’ current prices and availability (Booking.com and Vrbo) for the dates and number of travellers you chose. Final prices are always confirmed on the partner’s site.

    Who do I book with?

    Directly with the partner: Booking.com or Vrbo for places to stay, GetYourGuide for experiences, BookSurfCamps for surf camps and DiscoverCars for cars. Cancellation terms are the partner’s.

    Can I share the plan with my group?

    Yes. Use the “Share” button: anyone who opens the link sees the same plan, with the same dates.
