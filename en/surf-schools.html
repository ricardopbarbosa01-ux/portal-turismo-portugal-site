How we check

Before a school joins the list, we confirm these points. Checking is free.

  • Registration in Turismo de Portugal's RNAAT register, where the activity requires it
  • Address and Google Maps listing
  • Beaches where the school teaches
  • Certifications the school states, such as the Portuguese Surfing Federation (FPS)
  • Working website, phone and social accounts
  • Direct contact with the school

If you spot a wrong detail, tell us and we will fix it. The full criteria are in our editorial methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Do schools pay to be here?

No. The list is editorial and being on it is free. In the future a school may pay for extra visibility, such as appearing first in its area; when that happens it carries a “Partner” label. Checking is free and the same for every school.

Which school is best for beginners?

Filter by region and check the beaches each school teaches at. In summer the sea on the Algarve's south coast is usually calmer, which helps beginners.

How do I book a lesson?

Directly with the school, by website or phone. We don't charge anything or take a commission from schools on this list. If you'd rather pay online, you can book a lesson on GetYourGuide (affiliate link).

Booking with the school or on GetYourGuide: what is the difference?

With the school, you arrange everything with the people who teach. On GetYourGuide you compare lessons from several schools, see prices and reviews and pay online. Those lessons are not checked by us and, if you book there, we earn a commission; you pay the same.

I run a school. How do I get listed?

Request a free listing in the form above. We check the details and reply by email.
