How we verify

Before a school joins the list we check these points. Verification is free and the same for everyone.

  • RNAAT registration with Turismo de Portugal, where the activity requires it
  • Address and Google Maps listing
  • Beaches where the school teaches
  • Certifications the school states, such as the Portuguese Surfing Federation
  • Website, phone and social media working
  • Direct contact with the school

Frequently asked questions

Do schools pay to be here?

No. The list is editorial and being on it is free. In future a school may pay for more visibility, such as appearing first in its area; if that happens it carries the “Partner” label. Verification is free and the same for all.

Which school is best for beginners?

Use the region filter and check the beaches each school teaches at. In summer the south coast of the Algarve is usually calmer, which helps beginners.

How do I book a lesson?

Directly with the school, through its website, by phone or on social media. Four schools also sell lessons on GetYourGuide: you pay online there and, if you book that way, the portal earns a commission; the price is the same for you.

What is the difference between booking with the school and on GetYourGuide?

With the school, you arrange everything with the person who teaches. On GetYourGuide you see prices and reviews and pay online, under their cancellation rules.

I run a school. How do I join the list?

Request a free listing with the form above. We check the details and reply by email.
