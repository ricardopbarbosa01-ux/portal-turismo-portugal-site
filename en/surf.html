Before you go

When to go

The North Atlantic sends regular swell from October to April. In summer the sea calms down and the water warms up: the best time to learn.

Meet the shapers

Three kinds of wave

  • Beach break

    Sandy bottom. More forgiving and the best place to learn.

  • Reef

    Rock or lava bottom. Powerful, fast wave for experienced surfers.

  • Point break

    The wave wraps around a headland and peels long and regular — great to progress.
