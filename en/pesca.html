Before you go

Fishing licence

At sea

Issued by DGRM and requested online at the Balcão Eletrónico do Mar. Types: shore, boat, spearfishing or all; monthly or annual.

bmar.pt

Rivers and reservoirs

Inland-water fishing needs its own licence, issued by ICNF.

icnf.pt

Rules that matter

  • Closed seasons — some species, such as shad and lamprey, cannot be caught at certain times of year.
  • Minimum sizes — fish under the legal size go back in the water.
  • Protected areas — Ria Formosa, Arrábida and the south-west coast have their own rules.
  • Daily limits — there are catch limits per angler and gear limits per line.

Guidance only. Rules change — always check DGRM (sea) and ICNF (inland waters) before each trip.
