20 years on the other side of the desk

For 20 years I ran hotel operations in the Algarve and in Dublin: at Four Seasons, Pine Cliffs, IHG and Carvoeiro Clube, where I was director of operations.

Every morning, someone asked at reception: “How is the sea today? Which beach should we go to?”

And every day I watched the surf school, the beach restaurant and the fishing boat next door stay invisible, because the big booking sites don’t show them.

In December 2025 I left hotels. In January 2026 I started Portal Turismo Portugal with Kaptar.Studio, to give anyone, at any time, the answer I used to give at reception.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free? What is the catch?

There is no catch. The portal earns commissions on bookings made through our links (your price is the same) and from local businesses that pay to be featured, always labelled "Partner".

Do I need an account?

No. Beaches, webcams, tides and the planner work without signing up.

How much will Pro cost and when is it out?

We don't know yet and would rather not make it up. People on the list get an email when there is news.

What do you do with my email?

We keep it only for the Pro notice. We do not share or sell it. To leave, just reply to the email.

I run a business. What exactly am I paying for?

Visibility with people who are choosing a beach: first place in your zone, your own page and tracked links you can see in your own site's Analytics. We do not sell guaranteed bookings. The first 30 days are free.

Where does the sea data come from?

Waves, wind and water temperature come from the Open-Meteo model. Tides come from the Instituto Hidrográfico. The webcams belong to MEO; we only link to them.

Where do the numbers on this page come from?

The site's numbers are counted in our database (9 October 2026). The tourism numbers are from INE and Banco de Portugal, with the link next to each one.
