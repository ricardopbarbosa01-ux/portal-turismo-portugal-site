Frequently asked questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?

There isn't one. We earn commissions on bookings made through our links (you pay the same) and, in future, from local businesses that pay for a featured spot, always labelled "Partner".

Do I need an account?

No. Beaches, webcams, tides and the planner work without signing up.

How much will Pro cost and when?

We don't know yet and won't guess. People on the list get an email when there's news.

What do you do with my email?

We keep it only for the Pro notice. We don't share or sell it. To be removed, just reply to the email.

Where does the sea data come from?

Waves, wind and water temperature come from Open-Meteo. Tides come from Portugal's Hydrographic Institute. Webcams belong to MEO; we only link to them.
