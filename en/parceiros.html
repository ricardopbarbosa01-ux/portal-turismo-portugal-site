Frequently asked questions

How many people will see my business?

The portal is new. In October 2026 it gets about 600 clicks a month from Google, most of them to the webcams. That is why the price is low and the first 30 days are free: you see the visits in your own Analytics before you pay anything.

Is there a contract or a minimum term?

No. The yearly plan is paid once and the monthly one is cancelled with an email. When you cancel you go back to the free listing and keep it.

How do I pay? Do you take a commission on bookings?

By invoice, after the 30 free days and only if you want to continue. No online payment and no commission: you pay a fixed amount and every booking is yours.

Does paying get me into the guides or a good review?

No. The guides are not for sale and paid placements are always labelled Partner.

Why only one Founder per zone?

Because that is what the Founder buys: being the only featured business in the zone. Any number of Local Partners can join the same zone.

My business is not a surf school. Can I join?

Yes. Today the published directory is surf schools. For fishing, boat tours and experiences, ask for the preview: the proposal tells you where your business appears before you pay.
