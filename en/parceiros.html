Questions

Prefer to write? ola@portalturismoportugal.com

Is there a contract or minimum term?

No. Yearly is paid once and monthly is cancelled with one email. When you cancel you go back to Base and keep your listing.

How do I pay? Do you take a commission?

By invoice, after the 30 free days and only if you want to continue. There is no online payment. No commission: you pay a fixed fee and every booking is yours.

How do I know it works?

Links to your website are tagged portalturismoportugal, so you see the visits in your own Google Analytics. Each month we send you a summary of the clicks. We promise visibility, not bookings: €149 a year is about 2 to 4 group lessons.

Does paying get me a place in the guides or a good review?

No. Guides are not for sale. Paid placements always carry the Partner label.

My business isn't a surf school. Can I join?

Yes. Today the published directory is the surf schools one. For fishing, boat trips and experiences, get the preview: the proposal tells you where your business would appear, before you pay anything.

The site is young. Why pay now?

You don't pay now. The first 30 days are free, with no invoice. We show our real Google Search Console numbers, and tracked links let you check everything yourself.
