WHO EDITS PORTUGAL TRAVEL HUB

Ricardo Barbosa

Founder & Editor

View full profile on LinkedIn →

20 years in premium hospitality across Portugal and Ireland

I started my career in hospitality young, on the Algarve coast. Over two decades I worked at some of the most demanding operations in the segment — Four Seasons, InterContinental, Pine Cliffs Resort — learning what separates competent service from memorable service. My time in Ireland gave me perspective on how northern European coastal destinations attract the same kind of traveller who seeks out the Portuguese coast.

More recently, I served as Director of Operations at Carvoeiro Clube Group until the end of 2025, with cross-functional responsibility over short-term rental operations, F&B and guest experience in the heart of the Algarve. That is where I saw up close the problem Portugal Travel Hub exists to solve.

Why I built Portugal Travel Hub

I spent 20 years on the host side of hospitality. I saw the frustration of guests arriving after booking blindly through platforms that rank by commission, not by quality. I also saw exceptional operators — small surf schools, fishing outfits, boutique hotels — remain invisible because they do not pay enough to appear in the top spots.

Portugal Travel Hub is an editorial response to that imbalance. We charge for the verification we do — never for placement. The operators we recommend are editorially verified against criteria we keep public on our editorial methodology page.

This is a solo, full-time operation since January 2026. No investors. No editorial managers pushing partnerships. Just me, 20 years of experience, and the commitment that what I recommend is what I would recommend to a friend.
